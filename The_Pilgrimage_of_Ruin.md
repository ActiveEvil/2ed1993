# The Pilgrimage of Ruin

On the Hive World of Avellorn, there are rumours of children disappearing, animal mutilations, and esoteric symbols daubed on walls. Inquisitor I'mard Bruclee makes planetfall to investigate these strange goings-on. Could this be the work of an insidious Genestealer Cult, a cell of corrupted heretics, or just the ravings of chem-addled Hive scum?

After several weeks of bribes, blackmail, and beatings, the Inquisitor has secured a fresh collection of teeth, fingers, and, most importantly, answers. As is too often the case, a ranking member of the Ecclesiarchy has fallen to the whisperings of Chaos. Through dark sermons, the fallen zealot Confessor Obadiah Richelianus is turning the populace toward the very heresy he once swore to destroy. This canker must be excised before it spreads.

Obadiah Richelianus's influence is growing, and he has mustered a formidable following of cultists and hangers-on. They march toward a shattered Cathedral in the wastelands beyond the Main Hive, a journey the cult calls the Pilgrimage of Ruin. What they plan to do when they arrive remains a mystery, but one thing is certain: they cannot be allowed to succeed. Inquisitor Bruclee has requisitioned loyal elements from across Avellorn to bolster his entourage, preparing to meet the heretics head-on.

## Game One: The Sacrificial Dagger

**600 points per side**
**Game length: 3 turns**

Inquisitor Bruclee's force hurries to stop the machinations of the Pilgrimage of Ruin. He has managed to acquire artillery support but can only afford to leave a small detachment to defend it. Richelianus's right hand, Father Crilly, has been dispatched to raid and disrupt the Inquisitor's back lines—he and his men plan to sell their lives dearly.

### Defender: A small detachment of Loyal Imperial forces

This army can be made up of any mixed Imperial force using the normal rules for allies but may not contain Inquisitor Bruclee himself, or any Space Marines (no commander in their right mind would leave such a potent force to simply defend some guns).

The defenders are given the **Hold the Line** mission card even if players choose not to include any bunkers.

### Attacker: A heretic raiding party led by Father Crilly

This army can be made up of units from the Chaos Cult army list but may only take allies from the Imperial Guard. The army must include Father Crilly, who has the **Acolyte** profile but may act as an independent character.

The attackers are given the **Dawn Raid** mission card.

### Special rules

No characters can be taken with more than 2 wounds, and no psykers may be taken with a mastery greater than 2.

### Win bonus

If the defender wins they gain access to one **Preliminary Barrage** (see Codex: Imperial Guard) in the next game, even if they would not normally have access to this rule.

## Game Two: The Ritual of Ruin

**1500 points per side**
**Game length: 4-5 turns**

It transpires that the Pilgrimage of Ruin seeks to complete a dark ritual—one that will enable Obadiah Richelianus to ascend to daemonhood. Should he succeed, doom awaits Avellorn. Inquisitor I'mard Bruclee leads a daring charge to halt the Pilgrimage and slay Richelianus before his heresy is fulfilled.

### Defender: The cult of the Pilgrimage of Ruin

This army can be made up of units from the Chaos Cult army list but may only take allies from the Imperial Guard. They may also take 1 squad from the Chaos Space Marine list at its minimum unit size. The army must include Obadiah Richelianus who has the **Demagogue** profile.

The defenders are given the **Take and Hold** mission card.

### Attacker: Loyal Imperial forces under the command of Inquisitor I'mard Bruclee

This army can be made up of any mixed Imperial force using the normal rules for allies. The army must include Inquisitor Bruclee, who has the **Master Inquisitor** profile.

The attackers are given the **Assassins** mission card.

### The Ritual

The defender may attempt to complete the **Ritual of Ruin** which will allow Obadiah Richelianus to assend to daemonhood. The ritual site is also the **Take and Hold** objective—if Obadiah Richelianus moves within 3” of this location the ritual begins. At the start of each player's turn, if the ritual is taking place the defender rolls a D6 to complete the ritual. If the roll is equal to or less than the turn number, then the ritual is complete and Richelianus assends and becomes a **Greater Daemon**. The defender may choose which **Greater Daemon** Richelianus becomes; however, if they have taken a deity-aligned squad of Chaos Space Marines, the **Greater Daemon** must also be aligned to that God. The points cost of the **Greater Daemon** is deducted from the defending player's 1500 points limit, even if they choose not to attempt the ritual.
